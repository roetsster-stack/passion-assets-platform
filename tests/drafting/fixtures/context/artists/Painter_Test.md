# Test Painter — in her own words

<!-- source metadata, not part of her text -->
- **Author:** Test Painter (the artist).
- **Document:** "Notes for the register" (Word), sent by email on 1 November 2026.
- **Covers:** two works — *Blue Field*, *Harbour at Dusk*.
- **Transcription:** verbatim; nothing added, nothing corrected.

---

***Blue Field*** 2019

I painted *Blue Field* over one winter in a cold studio. The blue is the blue of the bay in July, which I wanted to keep.

***Harbour at Dusk*** 2021

This work began as a drawing of the harbour wall. It is about waiting, and about the light that comes just before the lamps.
